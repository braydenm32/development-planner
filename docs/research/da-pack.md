# DA document pack: what a development application for a new house needs

The checklist on the DA pack's cover (Share › Plans › DA pack) comes from the sources below. The rows themselves are data in [`src/da/checklist.ts`](../../src/da/checklist.ts) (`DA_ITEMS`), each with the sources that list it, so a council's list can differ by adding or leaving out rows (`only`, `not`) and by its own words (`says`).

The pack is a concept pack for a town planner or designer, never a lodgement. The council's checklist on the day, and its staff, have the final word.

## How the rows are marked

- **In this pack**, with its sheet numbers: the site plan, floor plans, roof plan, elevations and sections, shadow diagrams, cut and fill plan, stormwater concept, and the checks summary (as the start of the SEE's table of controls).
- **Not drawn yet**, with what to add: one of those sheets the plan can't make yet (no walls, no roof, no cut or fill).
- **From someone else**, with who usually makes it: survey, BASIX, SEE, landscape plan, finishes schedule, waste plan, cost report, owner's consent, and for some councils the site analysis plan, driveway long section (only with a driveway), erosion and sediment plan and notification plan.
- **Hazard rows** (bushfire assessment, flood report, geotechnical report, heritage impact statement) appear only when the NSW planning maps (Site › Map) flag the lot for bushfire, flood, landslide or heritage. Without the map, the cover says they aren't known.

## What is unverified

- Central Coast's **notification plan**: the checklist names it with no detail of what it shows. Its row is marked with an asterisk on the cover.
- Every council's "always" against "if needed": the tick boxes didn't come through in the text extracts. Each row's condition is taken from the item's own words; where an item has none, it is listed as usually needed.
- With no council switched on, the list is the state's requirements plus the documents most of the four councils ask for; rows only one or two councils ask for (site analysis, erosion, notification, driveway long section) are left out.
